David Andreas Horvath

Language Enthusiast & HYPIA Polyglot

I have been interested for many years in learning new languages, new grammar structures and new alphabets.

I was raised bilingual in German and Burgenland Croatian. I belong to a minority that has spoken Burgenland Croatian since the 16th century; there are only about 55,000 to 100,000 of us worldwide. I learned the Cyrillic alphabet in school, and when I was in Moscow, I was able to read the signs even though I didn't understand everything yet.

This experience was the primary motivation for my interest in languages.

My journey started with:
  • 1) 🇷🇺 Russian - 5 years of learning
  • 2) 🇲🇰 Macedonian - 1.5 years of learning
  • 3) 🇮🇷 Persian - 2 years of learning
  • 4) 🇮🇳 Hindi - Learning at the moment
